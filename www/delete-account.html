注销 ChinaPaw 账号

Delete your ChinaPaw account

方法一：在 App 内注销（立即生效）

打开 ChinaPaw →「我的」或「设置」→「注销账号」→ 确认。账号、档案、证件照片、消息和推送令牌会立即删除。
In the app: Me / Settings → Delete account → Confirm. Your account, profile, ID photos, messages and push tokens are deleted immediately.

方法二：无法登录时，在此提交申请

If you cannot sign in, submit this form. We delete the account within 7 working days.

已收到申请，我们会在 7 个工作日内删除账号及相关数据。Request received. We will delete the account within 7 working days.

删除的数据 / What is deleted

档案、证件照片、聊天消息、面试邀请、推送令牌，企业账号还包括发布的岗位。法律要求保留的记录除外。
Profile, ID photos, messages, interview invitations, push tokens and, for employers, posted jobs; except records we must keep by law.
